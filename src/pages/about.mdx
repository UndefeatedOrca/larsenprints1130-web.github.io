## Bio

I'm currently a student at Liberty University, studying
 Mechanical Engineering and trying to make it through junior
  year. I'm born and raised from eastern Pennsylvania
   (lately Reading, PA, about an hour from Philadelphia)
    and I'm 19 years old.

I've had a few internships and other jobs you can read about on
 [my 'professional' page](/professional) but I think you can
  learn more about how I think and what I do by checking out
   [some of my projects](/projects). If you want to know more
    in particular about how things are going for me personally,
     see [Life Stuff](/life-stuff).

I am an imperfect guy, but I am redeemed by way of my savior and greatest friend Jesus Christ. I love my church community and am so fortunate to have a supportive and loving family (both by blood and by friendship) who do so much to help me be the best I can be.

## How I work

The reason I'm in Mechanical Engineering is more to do with
 what I don't know about my work than what I do. What I
  do know is this:

- I am naturally a very inquisitive person.
- I am technically-minded, especially with math and physics.
- I love to *make* things.

Those things amount to a great combination of the scientific
 method and iterative, rapid design. I find a problem, think of
  a solution, build it badly, analyze the problems, make the
   solution better, and repeat until the problem is solved.

### What I can actually do
My favorite method of manufacturing and testing my designs
 and projects is 3D printing, and I've been fascinated by it
  for around a decade now. I've owned my own printers for 5
   years now, using them for a wide array of projects and
    activites, ranging from personal to academic to professional.

I also have a background designing for and personally
 fabricating projects using many other manufacturing
  techniques, like

<div class="sorted cols-3">

- Woodworking
- Laser Cutting
- Laser Engraving
- Sheet Metal Fabrication
- Anodizing
- Waterjet Cutting
- Composites (Carbon Fiber)

</div>

I use CAD as the middle-man between my ideas and reality.
 I got my start in blender as far back as middle school,
  but I am now highly proficient with Solidworks, Autodesk
   Fusion, and Onshape - I've even used AutoCAD some. I have
    also recently begun my use of FEA for some projects, 
    specifically static structural simulations and topology 
    optomization using ANSYS.
